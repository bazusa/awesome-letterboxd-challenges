# Awesome Letterboxd Challenges

A collection of film-watching challenges from the Letterboxd community.

## Annual Challenges

- [52 Years in 52 Weeks 2026](https://letterboxd.com/kynky/list/52-years-in-52-weeks-2026/) – Watch 52 first-time films, each from a different year, before the end of the year. ([kynky](https://letterboxd.com/kynky/))
- [Criterion Challenge 2026](https://letterboxd.com/benvsthemovies/list/the-criterion-challenge-2026/) – Watch 52 films from the Criterion Collection, matching one film to each of 52 categories. ([benvsthemovies](https://letterboxd.com/benvsthemovies/))
- [Golden Anniversary Challenge 2027](https://letterboxd.com/xellence/list/golden-anniversary-challenge-1977/) – Watch films released 50 years earlier, starting with 1977. ([xellence](https://letterboxd.com/xellence/))
- [Honey, I Shrunk the Watchlist! 2026](https://letterboxd.com/nien/list/honey-i-shrunk-the-watchlist-challenge-2026/) – A 52-film challenge featuring various prompts designed to help you tackle and reduce your personal watchlist. ([nien](https://letterboxd.com/nien/))
- [Letterboxd Season Challenge 2026-2027](https://letterboxd.com/sethpaul/list/the-12th-annual-letterboxd-season-challenge/) – 33 weeks, 33 categories running from September to May. ([sethpaul](https://letterboxd.com/sethpaul/))
- [Sports Cinema Challenge 2027](https://letterboxd.com/xellence/list/sports-cinema-challenge-2027/) – A sports movie challenge with tiered completion based on Olympic medals: 🥇 48 films (one from each category); 🥈 24 films (choose from available categories); 🥉 12 films (choose from available categories). ([xellence](https://letterboxd.com/xellence/))

## Monthly & Seasonal Challenges

- [Hooptober 2026](https://letterboxd.com/cinemonster/list/hooptober-the-13th-the-final-chapter/) – The annual October horror-watching challenge with strict genre and thematic constraints. (October) ([cinemonster](https://letterboxd.com/cinemonster/))
- [The Big Noirvember 2026](https://letterboxd.com/savagebob/list/the-big-noirvember-a-film-noir-challenge-3/) – Watch 30 film noir titles across 30 categories during the month of November. (November) ([savagebob](https://letterboxd.com/savagebob/))

## Other Challenges

- [Birth Year Challenge: Part 1](https://letterboxd.com/kynky/list/birth-year-challenge-part-1/) – Watch a set number of first-time watch movies all released in the year you were born. ([kynky](https://letterboxd.com/kynky/))
