# Awesome Letterboxd Challenges

A collection of film-watching challenges from the Letterboxd community.

## 📅 Annual Challenges

- [The Criterion Challenge 2026](https://letterboxd.com/benvsthemovies/list/the-criterion-challenge-2026/) – Watch 52 films from the Criterion Collection, matching one film to each of 52 categories. ([benvsthemovies](https://letterboxd.com/benvsthemovies/))
- [The Letterboxd Season Challenge 2026-2027](https://letterboxd.com/sethpaul/list/the-12th-annual-letterboxd-season-challenge/) – 33 weeks, 33 categories running from September to May. ([sethpaul](https://letterboxd.com/sethpaul/))

## 🌙 Monthly & Seasonal Challenges

- [Hooptober 2026](https://letterboxd.com/cinemonster/list/hooptober-the-13th-the-final-chapter/) – The annual October horror-watching challenge with strict genre and thematic constraints. (October) ([cinemonster](https://letterboxd.com/cinemonster/))
- [The Big Noirvember 2026](https://letterboxd.com/savagebob/list/the-big-noirvember-a-film-noir-challenge-3/) – Watch 30 film noir titles across 30 categories during the month of November. (November) ([savagebob](https://letterboxd.com/savagebob/))

## ♾️ Ongoing Challenges

- [Birth Year Challenge: Part 1](https://letterboxd.com/kynky/list/birth-year-challenge-part-1/) – Watch a set number of first-time watch movies all released in the year you were born. ([kynky](https://letterboxd.com/kynky/))
